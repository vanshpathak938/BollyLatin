01 / THE IDEA
TWO WORLDS.

SAME STAGE.

BollyLatin brings Indian and Latin dance communities together for one high-energy day of movement, culture, competition, and connection.

We are not trying to turn two dance traditions into one. We are putting them side by side — giving each its own identity while creating something new together.

05 / COMMUNITY
DANCE WITH A PURPOSE.

BUILT FOR THE COMMUNITY.

BollyLatin is more than a dance event. It is an opportunity to bring people together while supporting community-focused work.

Through competition registration, applications, merchandise, partnerships, and the event experience, the goal is to create a sustainable fundraiser while celebrating the people and cultures that make Charlotte's dance community special.
